# Target Modes

These are priority guides, not fixed section orders. Adapt to the actual record.

## General Academic CV
Purpose: maintain a credible master academic record.

Typical priorities:
1. Research Profile / Research Interests
2. Education
3. Research Experience
4. Research Papers / Publications
5. Current Research Agenda (if mature)
6. Awards
7. Methods
8. Academic Leadership / Service
9. Other relevant professional experience

## PhD / Research Graduate Application
Emphasize:
- coherent research identity;
- independent or increasingly independent research;
- research outputs;
- current research agenda if genuinely developed;
- relevant methods;
- intellectual trajectory;
- education and research-relevant awards.

Compress unrelated professional/extracurricular detail.

## Research Assistant Application
Emphasize:
- substantive fit with the project/professor;
- concrete methods already used;
- evidence/data/document work;
- coding/software/language capabilities when relevant;
- research-assistance experience;
- reliability-indicating project outputs.

Do not claim methods the applicant has not used merely because the job description mentions them.

## Scholarship Application
Emphasize, where relevant:
- academic performance;
- research achievement;
- competitive awards;
- leadership;
- university/community service;
- evidence of sustained contribution.

Do not turn scholarship tailoring into a generic leadership résumé if the award is academically focused.

## Conference / Academic Opportunity
Emphasize:
- the relevant paper/project;
- research question and fit;
- methods;
- current research identity;
- prior presentations or research recognition when relevant.

## Target-aware editing rule
Tailoring changes selection, emphasis, ordering, compression, and terminology. It does not change facts.
